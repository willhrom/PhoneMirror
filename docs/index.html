Get started in a minute

There's nothing to install on your phone. You only switch on one setting, once.

Download and open PhoneMirror.exeIt asks to add itself to your Start menu and desktop, then downloads its mirroring engine (about 10 MB).
Turn on USB debugging on your phoneSettings › About phone › tap Build number 7 times. Then Settings › System › Developer options › turn on USB debugging. (Samsung: About phone › Software information.)
Plug in your phone and tap "Allow"Tick Always allow from this computer. Your phone opens on the laptop right away, and from then on it opens whenever you plug in.
Unplug whenever you likePhone Mirror switches to Wi-Fi on its own when the phone and laptop share a network. On Android 11+ you can also pair by scanning a QR code with no cable at all.
Windows might say "Windows protected your PC" the first time, because the app isn't code-signed yet. Click More info → Run anyway.

What it does

Built on the open-source scrcpy engine, so it's fast and low-lag.

Opens by itself

Plug in your phone and the mirror appears. With "Start with Windows," it waits quietly in the tray.

USB or Wi-Fi

Pull the cable mid-use and it carries on over Wi-Fi. Pair by QR code on Android 11+.

Looks like a phone

A phone-shaped window in black, silver, or blue, with buttons for power, volume, back, home, and recents.

Full control

Click, type, scroll, and paste. Drag an APK in to install it, or any file to copy it to your phone.

Sound, too

Hear your phone's audio through your laptop (Android 11+).

Record and screenshot

Save MP4 recordings and PNG screenshots of your phone with one click.

Screen-off mode

Keep the phone's display dark to save battery while you use it from the laptop.

Updates itself

When a new version comes out, the app offers a one-click update.
